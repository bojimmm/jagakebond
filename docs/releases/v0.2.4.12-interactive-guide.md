# Interactive first-use guide

> **Release** 0.2.4.12 · **Status** shipped

Release candidate: 0.2.4.12 / Android versionCode 40 / cache jk-v46. Built on 0.2.4.11.

The five-card modal guide is replaced by an in-place guide. Each step dims the app around one real control and explains it in a card placed beside it (below, above, or to the side on the desktop rail and on a phone held sideways), never over it.

Steps (11; the "Hasil dan Foto lagi" step only when the account has a plant): Selamat datang, Lokasi dan cuaca, Ringkasan tanaman, Kamera, Riwayat, Cari dan saring, Hasil dan Foto lagi, Peta, Tandai petak kebun, Akun saya, Siap mencoba.

- Interactive: on the Riwayat and Peta steps the farmer can tap the highlighted button itself; it opens that screen and the guide continues there. "Lanjut" does the same.
- The camera is never opened by the guide (no permission prompt); the last step offers "Ambil foto sekarang", which closes the guide and opens the camera only when tapped.
- Closing (Lewati, Selesai, Escape, Android back) returns to the screen the guide started on and leaves no view scrolled.
- Storage key is now jk-tour-2, so every account, including those who finished the old guide, sees the new guide once. Replay stays in Akun → Panduan penggunaan.
- Text 13 px or more, buttons 44 px or more, 12/8 corners, transform/opacity motion only, reduced-motion respected.

Test suite: review/browser-tools/tour-0252.cjs (replaces tour-0239.cjs in the release regression).
