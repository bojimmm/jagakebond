# Phone acceptance — 0.2.4.19

> **Release** 0.2.4.19 · **Status** shipped

Phone acceptance means the owner installs the update over the existing Android app and checks real device behavior. Automated browser/JVM checks cannot certify the camera, GPS, Google account flow or field readability on that phone. Do not uninstall or clear app data.

- Confirm version 0.2.4.19, existing sign-in/history and correct logo.
- Capture a photo and import one from the gallery. Check preparation, manual pin, one GPS recenter button, place/elevation lookup and the separate clean original/display stamp.
- Interrupt connectivity and resume a queued upload. Confirm visible failure/retry without losing the photo or switching accounts.
- Re-analyse a saved/failed photo. Confirm visible progress, same plant/photo identity, preserved prior result if it fails, and the new result after success.
- Review short coexisting disease/pest labels, farmer edits, action-first bullet report, copy action and uncertainty. Open the chemical reference only after consent; confirm no unverified dose or treatment eligibility is claimed.
- Check Riwayat, a plot summary, daily-note save/failure/retry, map retry, sidebar and the five-step guide/replay in portrait and landscape.
- In the admin console, check route listing separately from the explicit synthetic image/JSON test. This test can incur a small provider request; it is not a diagnostic benchmark.

Record device/Android version, result and screenshots of any failure. Avoid including private farmer photos, access tokens or precise locations in shared evidence. Physical acceptance and agronomist review remain pending until performed and recorded.
