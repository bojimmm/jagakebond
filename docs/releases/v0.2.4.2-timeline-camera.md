# Timeline camera sizing (0.2.4.2)

> **Release** 0.2.4.2 · **Status** shipped

The Riwayat tree card and the timeline header previously used separate camera
markup. The card had the owner-requested 56 x 56 px target and 26 x 26 px glyph,
while the timeline inherited the older generic 44 px icon button.

Both surfaces now use the same `tree-cam` control treatment. The timeline camera
keeps its primary action styling and `Foto lagi` action, but matches the card at
56 x 56 px with a 26 px glyph. The browser audit measures both controls at 390 px
and 1440 px; the focused suite passes 42/42 checks.
